# btu lab pages

this is the HTML version of the btu lab site. I split the information across six pages instead of putting it all in one.

- `index.html` - home and visitor info
- `tools.html` - equipment and training rules
- `people.html` - staff, emeritus, and friends
- `visit.html` - getting there, access, and safety
- `events.html` - orientations, workshops, and the calendar
- `faculty-support.html` - teaching resources and ways to get involved
