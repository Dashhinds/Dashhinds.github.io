Visit the Lab and Know the Rules

Getting Access

  1. Read the basic expectations and decide what tools your project needs.
  2. Attend the approximately 20-minute general orientation.
  3. Check whether a separate tool orientation is required.
  4. Ask staff before using equipment that is unfamiliar.

The lab aims to welcome many kinds of makers. It is currently unable to sponsor BuffOne Guest Cards because of campus security requirements.

Rule One: Be Cool

Being cool means protecting the people, projects, equipment, and larger community around the lab.

  • Respect other people, their space, and their work.
  • Follow safety practices for yourself, other people, and the equipment.
  • Report equipment problems instead of hiding or repairing them yourself.
  • Do not use slang or pop-culture references as an excuse to insult people based on race, age, gender, sexual orientation, physical ability, or another identity.
  • Tell staff when you use the last of a shared item.
  • Ask permission before removing a tool or material from the lab.
  • Protect work surfaces from hot glue, paint, and other damage.
  • Keep common work areas clear when possible.
  • Return things to the place where they belong.
  • Tell staff when somebody is not treating the community well.
  • Clear scraps from tables and sweep scraps from the floor.
  • Keep the lab clean.
  • Use tools, materials, and processes as intended, and get trained.
  • Avoid any other behavior that harms the BTU, ATLAS, CU, or Earth community.

Policies

  1. BTU follows the University of Colorado policies and codes of conduct.
  2. People who repeatedly disregard the rules or disrespect others can lose access to the lab.

Specialized Tool Reminders

Wood Shop

A separate orientation is required regardless of previous experience. Do not work alone or use a machine that you do not understand.

Laser Cutters

A separate orientation and guided practice are required before independent use.

3D Printers

Use current BTU procedures, approved materials, and PrusaSlicer. Work with staff instead of self-training.

Shaper CNC

Complete a brief training and contact Zack Weaver before using this precision tool.

Read the complete tool and equipment information.

Shared Materials

BTU is not a free supply store. Makers are asked to give something back by displaying work, tagging the lab, offering a workshop, organizing supplies, or making another contribution.
