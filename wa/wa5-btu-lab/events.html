Orientations

General Orientation

New members begin with an approximately 20-minute orientation about community expectations, safety, and basic equipment.

Wood Shop Orientation

Registration is required. The calendar lists sessions led by Mischief Makers.

Laser Cutter Orientation

The calendar lists required laser orientations. Guided appointments continue until a maker can use the machine independently and correctly.

3D Printing Orientation

A one-time orientation takes about 10 to 15 minutes and can be requested for an individual or a class.

Shaper CNC Training

A brief training is required. Contact Zack Weaver to get started.

Host a Workshop

BTU welcomes skill-sharing workshops from students, instructors, local artists, and other community members.

  1. Complete the BTU workshop proposal form.
  2. Email atlasbtulab@gmail.com to say that the form was submitted.

The lab will follow up with confirmation.

Check the Calendar Before Arriving
