Faculty and Support

Faculty Planning

BTU exists to support student, faculty, and staff coursework, research, and experimentation. The lab reports supporting as many as 7,000 coursework hours in a semester.

Because demand is high, students and faculty complete brief, required orientations. Faculty teaching a class that will use BTU should submit the planning form every semester.

Open the BTU for ATLAS Courses planning form.

Questions can be sent to zack.weaver@colorado.edu.

Plan Training by Tool Area

Laser Cutting

Laser access requires a one-time orientation. Faculty should arrange class support through instructors, learning assistants, or teaching assistants and use the calendar for orientation scheduling.

3D Printing

All users need a one-time orientation of about 10 to 15 minutes. Individual or class sessions can be requested.

Wood Shop

Every user needs a one-time wood shop orientation, even with previous experience. Wood shop safety training must be completed with BTU staff.

Start Planning Early

Faculty should identify needed tools, project deadlines, and who will provide instruction before students begin an assignment. Early planning protects safety and helps the lab manage demand.
